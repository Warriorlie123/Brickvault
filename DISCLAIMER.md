## No real money, no real prizes

- All currency in the game is fictional. It cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or
  exchanged for anything of value.
- There are no purchases, ads or payments of any kind.
- Nothing you "win" in the game exists outside of your browser.
- Save data is stored only in your browser's local storage. No account,
  no server, no tracking.

## Not financial advice, and not an endorsement of gambling

The game deliberately includes mechanics that resemble real-world risk:
blind boxes with a house edge, loans with interest, a fluctuating market
and live auctions. They are there to be **understood**, not imitated.
The write-ups in [`docs/`](docs/) explain the math behind them and why,
in the long run, the house wins.

If gambling is causing problems for you or someone you know, in the US you can call or
text **1-800-GAMBLER**, or visit [ncpgambling.org](https://www.ncpgambling.org/).

## Market values

In-game values are based on approximate BrickLink "new" prices collected in
October 2026 (via Brickset). Values for Epic and Legendary figures are
deliberately multiplied for gameplay (see `content/HOW_TO_ADD_DATA.md`).
The raw values and their source pages are in `content/sources/`.
They are **not** price quotes and should not be used to buy or sell real items.
